Privacy

Finnian uses a small app, connected to the official Instagram API, for one thing: sending a single message to people who comment a keyword (like SEND) on a Finnian post asking for a link.

What it reads: the comment, your username and the comment ID, only on Finnian's own posts.

What it does: sends you one message with the link you asked for. No follow-ups, no selling, no sharing your info with anyone.

What it keeps: a record of which comments got a reply, so nobody gets messaged twice. Nothing else.

Email list: if you sign up on finnian.ca, your email is used only for Finnian news, and you can unsubscribe from any email.

Send music: if you send a track on finnian.ca/send-music, your artist name, email, link and note are kept only so finnian can listen and reply.

Deleting your data: email contact@finnian.ca and it's removed within 7 days.
